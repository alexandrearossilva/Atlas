## Purpose

This document stores ideas, improvements, and future features outside the current sprint. The Backlog prevents scope creep and protects the MVP. Items here are not automatically approved—each requires evaluation before implementation.

## Backlog Rules

- New ideas must be registered here
- Current sprint must never be interrupted
- Backlog items require approval before implementation
- Priority may change over time
- Completed items must be removed and registered in the Change Log

---

## Core Principle

The Backlog preserves focus. Ideas are valuable; execution is essential.
