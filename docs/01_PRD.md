# Project Atlas

## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Version: 1.0

Status: Approved

---

# 1. Project Overview

Atlas is a digital platform dedicated to preserving, organizing and presenting the greatest football goals in history.

Instead of functioning as a traditional news portal, Atlas is designed as a structured and searchable database where each goal becomes a permanent digital record connected to players, clubs, competitions, rankings and historical information.

The platform is designed to grow continuously while maintaining simplicity, organization and high-quality content.

---

# 2. Vision

Become the largest and most organized football goals platform available on the internet.

Atlas should become the primary reference for football fans searching for memorable goals, historical moments and curated rankings.

---

# 3. Mission

Organize football history through goals.

Deliver reliable information, excellent navigation and an engaging user experience.

---

# 4. MVP Objective

The first version (MVP) aims to validate the platform.

The MVP must allow visitors to:

• Browse rankings

• Access goal pages

• Navigate through players

• Navigate through clubs

• Navigate through competitions

• Search the available content

The MVP prioritizes quality over quantity.
