==========================================================
EDITORIAL STYLE
==========================================================

Do NOT use Lorem Ipsum.

Write content in Brazilian Portuguese.

Use a conversational tone.

The writing style should feel like someone passionate about football telling great stories.

Think:

A knowledgeable football fan.

Natural.

Light.

Engaging.

Objective.

Respectful.

Avoid:

Academic writing.

Wikipedia style.

Excessive journalism.

Slang.

Sensationalism.

Example tone:

"Romário precisava de uma chance. Bastava uma. Dentro da área, poucos atacantes foram tão decisivos quanto o Baixinho."

This editorial voice should be consistent across every page.
